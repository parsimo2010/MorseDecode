---
name: review-cw
description: Independently transcribe Morse (CW) recordings, verify literal keyed text against acoustic evidence with a second reader, and audit benchmark labels or search unscored source intervals.
---

Give each reader anonymous mono WAVs. Keep the ID map, catalog, old transcripts, production decoder, previous reports and other readers' results with the coordinator. For a corpus directory run `python scripts/blind_pack.py AUDIO_DIR NEW_PACK` from this skill. Give readers only the WAV subdirectory. For wideband sources isolate carriers first, retaining a mapping from the rendered sample grid back to the capture and frequency. Work with the full-duration channel when a neighboring passage helps identify the sender, speed or fade.

Read the actual keying. The offline helper `scripts/read_marks.py` estimates a copy from the signal and provides timing evidence. Run `python scripts/read_marks.py WAV --output marks.json` for an adaptive envelope read. For a regular sender try `--clocked` to fit Morse patterns to complex keying transitions, and optionally supply `--frequency HZ` or `--dit-ms MS` from an independent measurement. Compare the time-stamped marks under both fits. Inspect weak or disputed intervals with `python scripts/view_keying.py WAV FIGURE.png --start 2 --end 6`. Pass `--tone HZ` to investigate a different carrier. The figure uses SciPy and Matplotlib. Listen, slow down playback, compare multiple bandwidths, or measure onset-to-onset spacing as needed. Preserve unconventional spelling, cut numbers and procedural signs. Use an uncertainty marker for missing evidence. Distinguish an incomplete character at a crop edge from a transmitted character.

Freeze a literal first copy for every clip. Have a fresh-context LLM derive its own copy from the audio, measured marks or an unlabeled spectrum before it sees the first one. Independent signal processing is particularly valuable for fades or interference. Match the two reads by anonymous ID, inspect differing symbols and the surrounding spaces, and record the reason for each adjudication. A plausible callsign, an expected English word or agreement between two runs of one algorithm cannot verify a doubtful mark. Identify unwitnessed or disputed portions explicitly.

Only then open the old catalog. Keep a per-clip record of the frozen reads, the decision, and any uncertainty. If a historical label cannot be confirmed, retain its provenance in the audit and score only verified passages for a verified-only comparison. For new material, examine the original time spans including weak or intermittent signals, publish only intervals with agreed copy, and retain a coverage record and uncertain attempts for future review. Lock new references before measuring the production decoder.
